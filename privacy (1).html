Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Playverse (“we”, “us”) is a studio that makes free multiplayer browser games, including Cricket Rush. This page explains what information we use when you play and why. By using Playverse you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account. We use your first name as your player name and your photo on the lobby page.
  • Game data: your player name, wins and games played, and the rooms, matches, picks and scores you take part in.
  • On your device: we store your theme choice (dark or light), your camera view choice in Cricket Rush, and your sign-in session in your browser’s storage so you stay signed in.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To run rooms, matches and tournaments, and show scores to the friends you play with.
  • To show your win and game counts.
  • To keep the games working and fix problems.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use your data for advertising.

Who can see your information

Other players in your room can see your player name and your game results. Your email address and photo are not shown to other players.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) stores sign-in and game data. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts our website. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data until you ask us to delete it. Room data may be removed when a room is closed.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time with the “Sign out” button.
  • You can clear stored data in your browser settings.
  • To ask us to see or delete your data, contact us at the email below.

Children

Playverse is not meant for children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
